ATC Console Wiring

A joystick for bay select, clutch and E-stop buttons, four bay LEDs and a buzzer on the UNO Q's 3.3 V MCU headers, wired with jumper cables. A mini breadboard supplies the shared 3.3 V and GND rails. The UNO Q's built-in 8×13 LED matrix is the bay dashboard and needs no wiring.

Wiring rules

  • LED resistors

    KY-011 boards often have no series resistor. Look for tiny parts labelled 151, 221 or 331 on the LED board. If there are none, put a 150–220 Ω resistor in series with each R and G line on the breadboard. Never drive a bare LED straight from a GPIO.

  • Power the joystick from 3V3, never 5V

    Its pin is labelled +5V, but connect it to the 3V3 rail. A0 and A1 are not 5 V tolerant, and 3.3 V also matches the ADC's full range.

  • Joystick directions

    The firmware reads the centre position at boot and emits a bay event when the stick passes about 70% of its travel: up 1, right 2, down 3, left 4. Push each way once after assembly; if a direction lands on the wrong bay, swap or invert the axes in firmware.

  • Wire all three button pins

    Connect S, + and − on both KY-004 buttons. Boards differ on whether pressing pulls S high or low, so the firmware reads the idle level at boot and treats the opposite level as pressed.

  • Check the silkscreen

    Pin order differs between module batches. Match the letters printed on each board (S, +, −, R, G), not the position.

  • One ground

    Every module's − pin goes to the breadboard GND rail, and the rail goes to a UNO Q GND pin. The buzzer's middle pin is unused.
